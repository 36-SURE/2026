Overview

  • In this presentation, we are analyzing chocolate ratings around the world.

  • Chocolate is important because it is a favorite food of many people around the world.

  • We use a dataset that contains expert reviews of specialty chocolate bars from manufacturers around the world.

  • Each observation represents an individual chocolate bar review identified by a unique reference number.

  • We want to explore what makes a chocolate bar highly rated.

  • To do so, we will use plots and clustering.

  • We find rich and creamy chocolates are most highly rated.

Chocolate: the world’s most popular sweet treat

  • The history of chocolate began with the Maya, who were probably the first people in South America to cultivate the cocoa plant (Verna 2013).
  • Since then, it has become a remarkably versatile ingredient, being used in drinks, desserts, beverages, and even savory food.


Research Question: What are the characteristics of highly rated chocolate?

overall_avg_rating <- mean(chocolate$rating)

word_ratings |>
  filter(n>=50) |>
  mutate(overall_avg_rating = mean(chocolate$rating),
         rating_diff = avg_rating - overall_avg_rating) |>
  arrange(desc(rating_diff)) |>
  slice(c(1:5, (n()-4):n())) |>
  mutate(most_memorable_characteristics = fct_reorder(most_memorable_characteristics, rating_diff),
         diff_pos = if_else(rating_diff >= 0, TRUE, FALSE)) |>
  ggplot(aes(x=rating_diff,y = most_memorable_characteristics))+
  geom_col(aes(fill = diff_pos)) +
  geom_vline(xintercept = 0) +
  scale_fill_manual(values=NatParksPalettes::natparks.pals("DeathValley")) +
  theme_bw() +

Discussion

  • Despite chocolate often being characterized by its cocoa percentage, we do not find cocoa percentage to be indicative of rating.
  • Rather, chocolates described with memorable characteristics of rich and/or creamy, among others, were rated highest.
  • Future work may explore what aspects of these chocolates make them so delectable.
